The exam at a glance

WhoApplicants for a first multi-year residence permit (carte de séjour pluriannuelle), a first resident card (carte de résident), and naturalisation applicants
Format40 multiple-choice questions in French: 28 knowledge questions and 12 everyday-life scenarios (mises en situation)
Duration45 minutes maximum, on a tablet or computer, in an approved test centre
Pass mark32 correct answers out of 40 (80%)
RetakesAs many times as needed; the pass certificate does not expire

French language level

Besides the civic exam, a minimum level of French is required: A2 for a first multi-year residence permit, B1 for a first resident card (for applicants under 65), and B2 for naturalisation. Details and accepted diplomas: niveau de français exigé.

Registering for the exam

The exam is run by two organisations approved by the Ministry of the Interior: the Paris Île-de-France Chamber of Commerce (CCIP) and France Éducation International (FEI). Fees are set by each centre. Beware of websites selling “the real exam answers”: the ministry warns against fraud. Coq Civique is free and sells nothing. More: s’inscrire à l’examen civique.

Frequently asked questions

Is the exam in English?

No. The exam is in French only. That is why we recommend practising the questions in French, using the English explanations only to understand.

Is Coq Civique official?

No. Coq Civique is an independent and free website, not affiliated with the French Ministry of the Interior. The questions come from the official lists; the answers are written by Coq Civique.
